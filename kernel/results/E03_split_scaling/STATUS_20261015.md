# E03 H200 checkpoint

Full 20-case sweep completed at source commit c4bd484941402c67a9a8c879af352877b3e0a9db; clean worktree recorded in both smoke/full manifests. Both raw runs are preserved.

The numerical/comparator gate passed; this does not mean the performance hypothesis passed. V2/v1 throughput ratios range from 0.870 to 22.777. At B=32 they are 0.982 (8K), 1.048 (32K), 1.059 (64K), and 0.870 (128K). Thus split-KV greatly helps small batches in long contexts but does not universally solve high-batch scaling.

Comparison uses shared BF16 query/output dtype and identical packed bytes/scales. Historical v1 and v2 use different physical tensor axis orders, converted outside timing. The sweep uses synthetic packed data, not real-model KV statistics. Without a same-layout serial control and profiling, the speed difference cannot be attributed solely to split scheduling, and the remaining bottleneck cannot be uniquely separated into memory traffic, dequantization, or GQA rereads. Q/KV=1 only was measured.
